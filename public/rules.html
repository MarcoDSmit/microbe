Microbe Wars rules and glossary

Two players. Bacteria and Host compete for the most victory points.

Setup Your turn Tucks and reactions Site boons Scoring and winning Secret objective cards Glossary

1. Set up the match

  1. Choose Bacteria or Host. Each faction shuffles its own deck. Bacteria has 94 cards and Host has 72. Reserve cards are outside the draw decks.
  2. The game draws four distinct sites from a pool of twelve. Each site has six shared spaces.
  3. Draw six cards. Privately keep two of the three secret objectives offered. Objectives requiring a site can only appear when that site is on the board.
  4. The game randomly chooses who goes first. In Pass & Play, hand over the device whenever prompted, including for reactions.

2. Take your turn

  1. Resolve start-of-turn effects. You qualify for a site's boon if you start with at least three of your visible cards there and more than your opponent.
  2. Spend up to three actions. Each action lets you play one card, draw one card from your deck, or take the top card of your discard pile. You may repeat an action in any order.
  3. Play and pay. Choose Play or drag a card to a legal space. Pay by discarding the stated number of other cards from your hand. Click cards or drag them to the Payment area. A normal play at cost 0 still uses an action. Resolve any targets, abilities, and reactions before continuing.
  4. Resolve bonus plays. A bonus play spends no normal action. Pay the card's cost, applying any discount and restrictions stated by the ability.
  5. End turn. You may end early. If you have more than 12 cards, choose which to discard. If the match continues, your opponent takes the next turn unless Go Again grants you an extra turn.

Go Again grants one extra turn, even if it triggers more than once. It cannot trigger during that extra turn.

3. Resolve tucks and reactions

  • Tuck. Put the target beneath the card that tucked it. This opens the target's space. A local tuck targets the same site. A local sweep tucks all eligible targets there. Immune tucks and Ambush follow their printed target restrictions.
  • Antibiotic match strip. Find the antibiotic's symbol on the bacterium. Active (A) qualifies for a local tuck. Optimal (O) qualifies for a local tuck or lets an on-play attack choose one distant Optimal target in place of its local effect. Recurring attacks stay local. Apply any limits printed on the antibiotic.
  • Full-site tuck play. A card with an on-play tuck can use the temporary seventh space if there is an eligible local target. Choose a local attack. The new card takes a space opened by the tuck or by Refuge.
  • Refuge. When a card with Refuge would be tucked, its owner may move it to an open space at another site and cancel the tuck. The move uses no action and triggers no on-play abilities. Each copy can use Refuge once between the starts of its owner's turns.
  • Resistance (R). A matching R symbol blocks the tuck. In new matches, a visible bacterium with an unused reaction draws one after a matching antibiotic is played at its site and the attack resolves. B23 can tuck the antibiotic instead if it tucked nothing. B23 can also use its reaction to draw after another Host antibiotic play if B23 is still visible. Each copy gets one antibiotic reaction per game.
  • Suppress. While visible, H34 blocks bonus plays granted by bacteria played at its site. While visible, H36 blocks Bacteria from using Go Again anywhere.

5. Score the match

Check for the end of the match after a turn finishes. The game scores if either draw deck is empty, all 24 board spaces are filled, or one faction fills all six spaces at two sites.

  • Cards. Add base VP and applicable card and site synergies from visible cards. Add each visible card's printed tuck value for each card directly beneath it.
  • Territory. At each site, having more visible cards than your opponent earns 3 VP. Occupying all six spaces earns 5 VP total for that site. A tie earns neither faction territory points.
  • Secret objectives. Score each objective using its printed condition or formula. The in-game indicator updates as the board changes. Final points use the board when the match ends. Match-rail objectives may also require a recorded R reaction, an O or A tuck, or a distant O tuck. The relevant bacterium or its captor must still be visible, as specified.

The higher VP total wins. Equal totals are a tie.

During play, the row above your hand shows both factions’ public points. Each site has its own tally. The match replay includes the background log and records what a card contributed when its play resolved.

Choose Play again to start a fresh match with the same seats. The series tally counts Bacteria wins, Host wins and ties. The replay records the start of the first turn and the time of final scoring.

Glossary

Turn and hand Tucks and matches Abilities and reactions Sites and scoring

Turn and hand

Action
You get three per turn. For each one, play a card, draw one from your deck, or take the top card of your discard pile. You can repeat an action or end early.
Cost / free play
Cost is how many other cards you discard from your hand to play a card. A card that costs 0 still spends an action when played normally.
Bonus play
Play one extra card without spending a normal action. Pay its usual cost unless the effect gives a discount. “Cost −1” means one fewer discard. Follow any card or site restriction in the prompt.
Draw / recover / take top discard
Draw from the top of your deck. Recover means choose a card from your discard pile, unless the effect specifies a position. Take top discard returns only the top card.
Filter / draft
Filter means look at the stated number of cards from your deck, keep the allowed number, and discard the rest. Draft means choose from the secret objectives offered by the effect.
Hand limit
Start with six cards. At the end of your turn, discard down to 12 if needed.

Tucks and matches

Tuck / visible
Put the target beneath the card that tucked it. This opens its space. A visible card occupies a space on the board. Visible cards count toward control and can use their abilities.
Here / allied / opponent card
Here means this card’s site. An allied card is yours. An opponent card belongs to the other faction. In a scoring condition, “tucked here” means beneath the card that prints the condition.
Matching / eligible
A matching antibiotic target has an Active or Optimal symbol and meets the card’s targeting restrictions, such as Lung only. In a direct tuck, eligibility comes from the printed conditions. For example, B16 Ambush can tuck up to one opponent card here with no cards beneath it.
Local tuck / local sweep
Target cards at the same site. “ALL” tucks every eligible local target when you choose a local sweep. If an ability says “up to,” you can choose fewer targets or decline.
Full-site tuck play
An on-play tuck card with an eligible local target can use a temporary seventh space at a full site. You must choose a local attack. The played card moves into the space opened by the tuck or by Refuge.
Optimal / Active / distant tuck
Both Optimal and Active matches can qualify for a local antibiotic tuck. An antibiotic with an on-play attack can choose one distant Optimal target instead of its local attack. Recurring attacks stay local, and card-specific limits apply.
Resistance match
A matching R symbol blocks the tuck. In new matches, when Host plays a matching antibiotic at this site, draw 1 after the attack if the bacterium is still visible and its reaction is unused. Each copy can react once per game. B23 can tuck the local antibiotic instead if it tucked nothing, or spend its reaction to draw after another Host antibiotic play while B23 is visible. On C. diff, R represents an opening created by antibiotic disruption.

Abilities and reactions

On play / start of turn / while visible
On-play effects resolve when a card is played. Start-of-turn effects resolve at the start of your turn. A while-visible effect lasts while its card occupies a board space.
Reaction
An effect that responds to a trigger without using an action. In Pass & Play, hand the device over when the game asks the other player to react.
Refuge
When a card with Refuge would be tucked, you may move that card to an open space at another site and cancel the tuck. Moving triggers no on-play abilities. Each copy can use Refuge once between the starts of your turns.
Ambush
When played, you may tuck one Host card at this site that has nothing tucked beneath it.
Go Again
Take one extra turn after this one. Multiple triggers grant one extra turn total. Go Again cannot trigger during that extra turn.
Suppress
While visible, H34 blocks bonus plays granted by Bacteria played at its site. H36 blocks Bacteria from using Go Again anywhere.

Sites and scoring

Site / majority / full control
Four sites are drawn for each match, with six shared spaces each. Majority means you have more visible cards there than your opponent. Full control means all six spaces are yours.
Boon
Each site has its own perk, printed on the board. It unlocks when you start your turn with at least three of your cards there and more than your opponent. A 3–3 tie grants neither side a boon.
VP / base / synergy / tuck points
VP means victory points. Visible cards contribute their printed base points, applicable card and site synergies, and their printed tuck value for each card directly beneath them.
Reading compact synergy
“Liver +2 VP” scores two points if the card is visible at Liver. A slash joins alternative sites for one award. Separate clauses can both score. “Per pair” counts complete pairs, and “max” caps that clause. Site cards show their own tag bonuses.
Territory / secret objectives
At scoring, a majority earns 3 VP and full control earns 5 VP for that site. Secret objectives are private scoring goals. Keep two of your opening three. Some objectives ask you to keep cards visible after a specific match-rail reaction or tuck. An antibiotic’s original match and whether it tucked from another site determine the score, even if the antibiotic later moves.
End of the match
Score at the end of a turn if either draw deck is empty, all 24 spaces are filled, or one faction fills all six spaces at two sites. The higher VP total wins.
