Microbe Wars rules and glossary

Two players. Bacteria and Host compete for the most victory points.

Setup Your turn Tucks and reactions Site boons Scoring and winning Secret objective cards Glossary

1. Set up

  1. Choose Bacteria or Host. Shuffle each faction’s deck and draw six cards each.
  2. Draw four of the twelve clinical sites. Each site has four shared spaces. Compare the site icons on a Bacteria card with the icons on the board to find its synergy sites.
  3. Each faction privately keeps two of its three offered secret objectives. Objectives that require a clinical site can only appear if that site is active.
  4. The game randomly chooses who starts. In Pass & Play, hand over the device when prompted, including for choices made during the opponent’s turn.

2. Take your turn

  1. Check boons. You have majority at a site if at least two of your cards are visible there and you have more than your opponent. Resolve the boons for the sites where you start with majority. A 2–2 split gives neither side a boon.
  2. Spend up to three actions. Each action can play one card, draw one from your deck, or take the top card from your discard pile. Repeat actions in any order.
  3. Play and pay. Place your card in a legal space. Discard other cards from your hand equal to its cost. A normal play spends an action even at zero cost. Resolve the card’s powers and any reactions.
  4. Take a bonus play when offered. You may play one additional card at its usual discard cost without spending an action.
  5. End your turn. Discard down to 12 cards if needed. Your opponent goes next unless Go Again grants you an extra turn.

3. Tuck, Evade, and Resistance

  • Tuck. Move an eligible visible target and everything beneath it under the card doing the tucking. This frees a space and uses one target from the effect’s allowance. Bacteria and antibiotics can take any size stack. An Immune card can tuck a target with zero or one card beneath it.
  • Active (A) and Optimal (O). Match the antibiotic’s symbol to the bacterium’s strip. A qualifies at the same site. O qualifies at the same site and can also let an on-play antibiotic attack reach one target at another site. A visible antibiotic holding an O tuck it made is protected from being tucked.
  • Full-site tuck play. When all four spaces are occupied, an eligible on-play tuck uses a temporary fifth position. The played card then takes a space freed by the local tuck or an Evade.
  • Evade. When this card would be tucked, its owner may move it and its stack to an open space at another site and cancel the tuck. This move spends no action and triggers no on-play powers. Each copy may Evade once between the starts of its owner’s turns.
  • Resistance (R). If Host plays an antibiotic at a site with a visible bacterium showing its symbol under R, cancel the antibiotic’s effects. Tuck it under the matching bacterium and give Bacteria one deck draw. If several match, Bacteria chooses which one receives the antibiotic. Host still spends the play and pays the discard cost. Resistance can trigger repeatedly.

A 2–2 tie gives neither player a boon. Control is checked at the start of your turn. The board shows the boon and scoring synergies for each active site.

5. Score the match

At the end of a turn, score if either deck is empty, all sixteen visible spaces are filled, or one faction fills all four spaces at two sites.

Base VP
Add the printed base VP of each visible card.
Bacteria synergy
A visible bacterium earns +2 VP once if its current site matches an icon on its card.
Immune synergy
Each visible Immune card earns +2 VP per other visible Immune card at its site.
Tuck VP
A visible antibiotic earns +2 VP for every card in its stack and another +2 VP per O tuck it made and still holds. An antibiotic tucked by Bacteria contributes +1 VP to Host per card beneath it. Immune tuck points use the card’s tuck value. Played Host cards show their live tuck VP.
Territory
Majority is worth +1 VP. Filling all four spaces at a site with your faction earns +3 VP total.
Secret objectives
Apply each objective’s condition to the final board and any match history it requires.

The higher total wins. Equal totals are a tie.

Glossary

Turn and hand Tucks and matches Card powers Sites and scoring

Turn and hand

Action
One of three opportunities per turn to play, draw, or take the top discard.
Cost
The number of other cards you discard from hand when you play a card.
Bonus play
Play one more card at its usual discard cost without spending an action.
Draw +1 or +2
Take one or two cards from your faction deck. Peripheral IV’s boon may substitute a face-up discard for each draw.
Take top discard
Spend an action to return the top card of your discard pile to your hand.
Draft objective · 2 → 1
Draw up to two eligible secret objectives, keep one in addition to your existing objectives, and discard the other. If the pool is empty, gain nothing.

Tucks and matches

Visible
A card occupying one of the four shared spaces at its site.
Tuck stack
All cards held beneath a card, including inherited stacks. The stack moves with the card holding it.
Local / distant
Local means the same site. Distant means a different active site.
Active (A)
Qualifies for a local antibiotic tuck.
Optimal (O)
Qualifies for a local antibiotic tuck and can allow a distant on-play tuck. An antibiotic holding an O tuck it made earns the O bonus and is protected from being tucked.
Resistance (R)
A matching visible bacterium at the played antibiotic’s site cancels and tucks the antibiotic. Bacteria draws one.

Card powers

On play
Resolve the power when you play the card.
While visible
The power applies while the card occupies a board space.
Ambush
An optional tuck of one eligible Host card at this site. A full-site play requires an eligible local target.
Evade
Cancel a tuck by moving the card and its stack to an open space at another site.
Go Again
Take one extra turn after this one. Multiple triggers grant one extra turn total. An extra turn cannot generate another extra turn.
Block Go Again
While Doxycycline IV specialist or Burkholderia is visible, the opposing faction cannot take an extra turn.

Sites and scoring

Majority
At least two visible cards and more than your opponent at a site.
Full control
All four visible spaces at a site are occupied by your faction.
Boon
A site power available if you start your turn with majority there.
Synergy
Bonus VP from a Bacteria card at a matching site or from other visible Immune cards sharing a site.
Tuck VP
Points earned from cards in a tuck stack. Played Host cards show the current amount at the bottom of the card.
Territory VP
+1 for majority or +3 total for full control.
