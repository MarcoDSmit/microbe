Microbe Wars privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026. Microbe Wars is developed by Marco Smit. This policy describes the Android app and distinguishes its offline game from the separately hosted web game.

Offline Android game

The Android app runs Solo, Pass & Play, the tutorial and card library on your device. It requires no player account. The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK or automatic match upload.

The app stores saved matches, game settings, card-library progress and previous objective offers in local app storage. These records stay on your device during ordinary play. Android backup and device-transfer services may include app data according to your device and Google account settings.

Replay exports and reports

If you choose to export a match, the replay includes both factions’ cards, moves, objectives and scoring. You decide whether to share that file. The game does not upload it automatically or automatically use your matches to train its bots.

The Report a bug link opens GitHub. Reports you submit there are public and are handled under GitHub’s privacy policy. Please leave personal information and private match links out of public reports.

External websites and the web game

External links may open websites with their own privacy policies. Visiting a website sends ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to its operator. The separate hosted web game sends match actions and room credentials to its hosting service to operate online rooms. Those online rooms are separate from the offline Android modes.

Permissions and deletion

The Android app declares internet access to support web links. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or health records. You can clear saved game data through Android Settings or uninstall the app. Android backup copies are managed through your device and account backup settings.

Contact and changes

Contact the developer through the Microbe Wars support page. Privacy questions posted there are public, so do not include sensitive information. This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change, including if advertising or analytics are introduced.
